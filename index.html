What Jovia offers

Every module is visible on your dashboard after registration. Access stays locked until an administrator verifies your activation payment.

Watch celebrity videos

Creators, AI, music, podcasts, sports and entertainment. Choose watch time, press Start, and earn every second. Silver pays ₦1,500 ($1.50) for 30 seconds. Gold pays ₦3,000 ($3.00) for 30 seconds.

Play fun games

Choose play time, fund your wallet if needed, press Start. Every second counts — win or lose, time is rewarded. Silver: ₦3,000 ($3.00) / 60s. Gold: ₦6,000 ($6.00) / 60s.

Music streaming

Listen on Spotify, Apple Music, Audiomack or Boomplay through Jovia. Earn up to ₦1,000 ($1.00) on Silver or ₦2,000 ($2.00) on Gold per eligible session, with extra bonuses on longer streams.

Easy Buy

Shop, sell and earn in one place. Upload approved products and earn up to ₦7,000 ($7.00) on Silver or ₦13,000 ($13.00) on Gold.

Meta activities

Share posts, like content and connect WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram. Eligible tasks pay up to ₦2,000 ($2.00) on Silver and ₦3,000 ($3.00) on Gold.

Skill Verse

Learn social media, copywriting, UI/UX, YouTube automation, app development, graphic design and more while you keep earning on the platform.

Jovia Debit Card

Optional card for shopping, airtime, subscriptions and withdrawals. After activation, Silver members receive a $3 daily bonus and Gold members receive $6.

Friday Bonus Rewards

Every Friday a random number appears on Gold accounts. Tap it to claim $10 and withdraw instantly. Monthly validity applies. FBR is not active on Silver.
